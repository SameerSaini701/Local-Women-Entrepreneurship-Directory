About Local Women Entrepreneurship Directory

A platform created to celebrate, support and empower women entrepreneurs across Punjab and Haryana.

OUR JOURNEY

Our Story

Local Women Entrepreneurship Directory was created with a simple belief: every woman-led business deserves to be seen, recognised and supported. Across Punjab and Haryana, women are turning their ideas, creativity and skills into businesses. However, many local entrepreneurs still need greater visibility and opportunities to connect with customers. Through our platform, we aim to bring these businesses into the spotlight and encourage people to discover and support them.

Our Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To promote women entrepreneurs by increasing the visibility of their businesses and helping customers discover local women-led ventures.

Our Vision

To build a supportive community where women entrepreneurs can grow, connect and create new opportunities for themselves.

Our Values

We believe in equality, creativity, collaboration and supporting local businesses.

OUR PURPOSE

What We Aim to Achieve
